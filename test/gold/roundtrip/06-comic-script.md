# The Lighthouse Keeper's Daughter, Issue 1

## Page 1

### Panel 1

Wide establishing shot. A lighthouse on a black rock, rain slanting across the
beam. The sea is the colour of slate.

CAPTION: Three days before the storm.

### Panel 2

Interior. MIRA (17, oilskin coat two sizes too big) climbs the spiral stair,
lantern in hand.

MIRA: Papa? The glass is cracked again.

### Panel 3

Close on the lamp room. The great lens, one segment spidered with fractures.
Light bends wrong.

SFX: KRRK

MIRA (small): Not again.

## Page 2

### Panel 1 {grid: 2x2}

Four-panel grid. Top left: MIRA's hands on the brass. Top right: the keeper's
log, open, the last entry unfinished. Bottom left: a cup of tea gone cold.
Bottom right: the stair, empty.

CAPTION: He had not come down for supper.

### Panel 2

MIRA at the window. Far out, a light that is not a ship.

MIRA: ...

### Panel 3

Splash across the bottom third. The beam sweeps the water and catches something
enormous turning beneath the surface.

SFX: whoooom

MIRA (off): Papa!
